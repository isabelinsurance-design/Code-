1

Start a new campaign

2 minutes

  1. Open Ads Manager

    It opens in a new tab. Log in with your Facebook if it asks.

  2. At the top left, check that the ad account is the one you used for your last campaign.

  3. Click the green + Create (Crear) button.

  4. Choose Leads (Clientes potenciales) and click Continue (Continuar).

  5. If Meta asks how to set it up, choose Manual leads campaign (Campaña manual de clientes potenciales) so you can pick the cities and the language. Click Continue.

2

Campaign settings

3 minutes

  1. Campaign name: delete what's there and paste this.

  2. Find Special Ad Categories (Categorías de anuncios especiales). Choose Financial products and services (Productos y servicios financieros) and country United States. Meta requires it for insurance ads. Because of it, age and gender can't be chosen later. That's normal.

  3. If Advantage campaign budget (Presupuesto de campaña Advantage) is on here, choose Daily budget (Presupuesto diario) and type the amount. If Meta asks for the budget on the next screen instead, type it there.

  4. Click Next (Siguiente).

3

Ad set: where, when and who

8 minutes

  1. Ad set name:

  2. Conversion location (Ubicación de la conversión): choose Instant forms (Formularios instantáneos). This can't be changed later.

  3. Performance goal (Objetivo de rendimiento): leave Maximize number of leads.

  4. Facebook Page: choose your page.

  5. Schedule (Programación): start date and time, then turn on End date (Fecha de finalización).

  6. Locations (Ubicaciones): if "United States" is listed, remove it. Then add these cities one by one. After adding each one, set its radius in miles. Meta's minimum is 15.

  7. Languages (Idiomas), under Show more options (Mostrar más opciones): type and choose Spanish (Español). If you see "Spanish (All)", take that one.

  8. Age and gender are locked. That's the special category, not a mistake.

  9. Placements (Ubicaciones de los anuncios): leave Advantage+ placements. Click Next.

    7

    Review and publish

    2 minutes

    1. Look at the preview of each ad on the right: check that the text has no brackets [ ] and that the image looks right.

    2. Click Publish (Publicar) at the bottom right.

    3. Meta reviews the ads, usually within a day. They show In review (En revisión), then Active (Activo) from your start date.

    4. If one says Rejected (Rechazado), don't change things at random. Copy Meta's reason and send it to me in our chat. I'll fix the text.

    8

    Once it's running

    a few minutes a day

    1. Take a screenshot of each published ad and keep it, in case your FMO or a carrier asks.

    2. New leads appear in Meta Business Suite → All tools → Leads Center (Centro de clientes potenciales). Meta doesn't email them to you, so look a few times a day.

    3. Call each new lead within the first hour, between 8 am and 9 pm. On the call: the TPMO notice before any benefit, and the SOA before talking about specific plans. If they don't answer, send this text:

    4. Optional: Meta's lead testing tool sends a fake lead through your form so you can see how one arrives.

    5. Every Monday: write the spend and the leads from Ads Manager in the Seguimiento tab of the Excel. Your morning employee reminds you.

    9

    A new ad each week

    8 minutes each time

    On each date, do the same as step 6: duplicate an ad, change the name, the image and the texts, and publish. All of them stop on Dec 7.
