## Key Question 1: Top reasons phone enrollments get kicked back, pended, voided or flagged

### Takeaway
Across FMO guides, carrier checklists and forum threads, the recurring kick-back causes are: (1) missing/incomplete beneficiary data (especially MBI, DOB, physical address, Part A/B dates); (2) election-period problems — wrong or unsupported SEP code, attestation section not matching the agent-section election, missing SEP-required dates or Medicaid/LIS info; (3) no SOA (or SOA that doesn't cover the product discussed); (4) recording failures — not recording the whole call, beneficiary refusing recording, recording not retrievable when carrier/CMS asks; (5) missing or late TPMO disclaimer; (6) not using the carrier's approved telephonic process (unauthorized channel, missing voice-signature code); (7) not reviewing required benefits/disclosures before enrolling.

### Cited Findings

**Data/application completeness**
- **[FMO blog]** Ritter: "Any time an application gets flagged during the submission process, it's a delay on your clients' coverage and your commission." Tip 1 is to gather full name, contact info, DOB and MBI before starting, because "navigating away from an online application increases the risk of losing your spot or making a mistake." Ritter pushes online/e-app submission and a CRM to pre-store client data (prefilled "FastTrack" MAPD/PDP apps for e-signature). — [Ritter, 10 Tips to Submitting an Error-Free Medicare Enrollment Application](https://ritterim.com/blog/10-tips-to-submitting-an-error-free-medicare-enrollment-application/); updated version dated Dec 2025 at [Medicareful/Ritter](https://medicareful.ritterim.com/blog/10-tips-to-submitting-an-error-free-medicare-enrollment-application); podcast version: [Agent Survival Guide](https://agentsurvivalguide.podbean.com/e/10-tips-to-submitting-an-error-free-medicare-enrollment-application) ("double and triple check your enrollment applications for errors!").
- **[Agent-training site]** A complete MA/PDP enrollment request requires: name, permanent residence address (P.O. box alone is not acceptable), DOB, sex, phone number, MBI, Part A/B status, plan selection, premium payment option, ESRD status, and a signature/attestation with date. An incomplete request is not denied outright; the plan must first try to obtain the missing information (i.e., it pends). Default effective date is the first of the month after a valid request, but the election period governs. — [Open Exam Prep AHIP study guide, 10.2 Enrollment Procedures](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ahip-medicare/enrollment-disclosure-requirements/enrollment-procedures-application-requirements)
- **[Agency blog]** "The selected period in the Attestation of Eligibility section needs to align with the selection of IEP, AEP, or SEP in the Agent/Office Use section of the application. If the election period requires a date, like the date a client is losing coverage, make sure it's present and accurate." Also: "If the SEP requires any extra information such as Medicaid, PDP, or Extra Help info, it must be on the application." Review the completed app with the client before submitting. — [Essential Care Agents, 10 Tips for a Flawless Medicare Enrollment Application](https://essentialcareagents.com/blog/10-tips-for-submitting-a-flawless-medicare-enrollment-application/)
- **[Agency blog]** "The wrong SEP code can get the application kicked back as incorrect" and SEP codes have changed over time. — [GMIA, Medicare's SEP: Are You Using the Correct Codes?](https://gmiainc.com/medicares-sep-are-you-using-the-correct-codes/)
- **[Official]** CMS enrollment guidance: the SEP time frame "denotes the time frame during which an individual may make an enrollment or disenrollment request. It does not necessarily correspond to the effective date." — [CMS CY2025 MA/Part D Enrollment and Disenrollment Guidance](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/cy-2025-cd-enrollment-and-disenrollment-guidance.pdf-0)
- **[Forum]** Thread "I messed up a Medicare enrollment" — an agent suggests an SEP not yet active can be reused by resubmitting the correct plan (anecdotal). — [insurance-forums.com](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/i-messed-up-a-medicare-enrollment.113050/)

**SOA problems**
- **[Vendor blog]** "Scope creep": discussing a product type not on the SOA; "If the beneficiary asks about a product type not in the SOA, you must obtain a new SOA before discussing it." Cross-selling dental/vision/life during a Medicare call without SOA authorization is flagged. "A single missed disclosure or prohibited phrase can turn a successful enrollment into a compliance violation, a CTM complaint, and potentially a CMS audit." — [AgentTech, CMS-Compliant Enrollment Calls (Apr 11, 2026)](https://agenttech.io/blog/cms-compliant-enrollment-calls) (domain was unreachable; excerpt from search)
- **[Vendor blog]** Telephonic SOA (T-SOA) is verbal consent captured on a recorded call; the entire T-SOA conversation must be recorded and retained. Outbound calls generally needed the SOA 48 hours before the appointment; inbound beneficiary-initiated calls could capture it during the call. — [AgentTech, Telephonic Scope of Appointment (Mar 8, 2026)](https://agenttech.io/blog/telephonic-scope-of-appointment)
- **[FMO blog]** Quotit: "a missed SOA can derail your AEP season before it starts." — [Quotit, AEP Final Compliance Check](https://hub.quotit.com/blog/aep-final-compliance-check-for-agent)
- **[FMO blog]** Ritter FAQ: any MA/MAPD/PDP enrollment meeting not held in person must be recorded (telephonic and video), and an SOA must be collected before discussing MAPD options. — [Ritter, FAQs About the Medicare Call Recording Requirements](https://ritterim.com/blog/faqs-about-the-medicare-call-recording-requirements/)
- **[Podcast]** Insurance Producers Guild ep. 19: the three CMS rules generating the most agent violations are "Scope of Appointment requirements, full call recording obligations, and TPMO disclaimer rules." — [Buzzsprout, EP19 2027 Medicare Rules: Faster Sales, More Liability](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2603798/episodes/19522040-ep19-2027-medicare-rules-faster-sales-more-liability)

**Recording failures**
- **[Official]** CMS Agent/Broker Marketing FAQs (Oct 19, 2022): all marketing, sales and enrollment calls between a TPMO and a beneficiary must be recorded in their entirety, including Zoom/virtual-presence calls; in-person meetings need not be recorded; "If a beneficiary declines to have their call with a TPMO recorded, the sales agent must end the call" — no exceptions; CMS does not specify a recording tool and TPMOs "should work with the plans for whom they sell to determine the method/format of recording"; recordings must be HIPAA-compliant and producible when CMS requests a beneficiary's recordings. — [CMS Agent Broker Marketing FAQs 10-19-2022 (PDF)](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/agent-broker-marketing-faqs-10-19-2022.pdf)
- **[Trade assoc.]** NABIP/NAHU summary: "CMS plans on reviewing agent audio calls both based on consumer complaints and at random" and expects the entity maintaining the call to provide the recording on request. — [NABIP, CMS Provides Some Clarification…](https://www.nabip.org/membership-resources/medicare-portal/medicare-marketing-rule-resources/cms-provides-some-clarification-on-how-to-comply-with-medicare-marketing-final-rule)
- **[Forum]** An agent reports their phone system only keeps recordings 90 days, so they must download copies within that window (retention trap). — [insurance-forums, Call recording ridiculousness](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/call-recording-ridiculousness.108899/page-8)
- **[Vendor blog]** Recording should continue through holds, warm transfers, conference/three-way calls and transfers to enrollment teams; never pause mid-call. If a required call fails to record, notify agency leadership and/or the carrier and investigate; one source says report missing recordings to carriers within 48 hours of discovery. — [PSM Brokerage, Call Recording and Retention Requirements](https://www.psmbrokerage.com/blog/call-recording-and-retention-requirements-for-telephonic-sales-of-medicare-plans); [Thunai, CMS Call Recording Requirements in 2026](https://www.thunai.ai/blog/cms-call-recording-requirements)
- **[Vendor blog]** Common misconception: recording "only applies during the Annual Enrollment Period" — it is year-round. — [EnrollHere, CMS Call Recording Requirements](https://enrollhere.com/blogs/cms-call-recording-requirements)

**TPMO disclaimer**
- **[Carrier/GA doc]** Warner Pacific broker checklist: "Within the first 60 seconds of the call, you must state the following disclaimer, which depends on whether the agent represents all plans"; also confirm the call is recorded and secure verbal agreement. — [Warner Pacific, Broker Checklist Phone and Enrollments (PDF)](https://login.warnerpacific.com/getmedia/7f54fcd2-0365-408b-a279-5f82a168c8e5/Broker-Checklist-Phone-and-Enrollments.pdf)
- **[FMO blog]** The disclaimer "is a standardized disclaimer and must be used verbatim." — [PSM Brokerage, New TPMO Disclaimer](https://www.psmbrokerage.com/blog/medicare-marketing-changes-in-2024-new-tpmo-disclaimer)
- **[FMO/Vendor blogs]** Under the CY2027 rule the TPMO disclaimer must be given before any discussion of benefits (replacing the "first minute" timing). — [Agent CRM, Medicare Final Rule for Plan Year 2027](https://blog.agent-crm.com/medicare-final-rule-for-plan-year-2027-everything-agents-need-to-know/); [New Horizons, The 2027 CMS Final Rule](https://blog.newhorizonsmktg.com/the-2027-cms-final-rule-what-agents-need-to-know)

**Wrong channel / carrier-approved process**
- **[Forum]** "Which carrier's approved telephonic enrollment process are you using?" is the practical first question; "All of the carriers that I work with that accept telephonic enrollments already have a method to record them using their system, so no need to go through FMO for this. For example, UHC and Humana to name a few." Another (older) poster: UHC guidance required a signed SOA before a telephonic presentation and said field agents may not enroll electronically over the phone — "Only UnitedHealthcare-approved and authorized call centers are permitted to conduct telephonic enrollments." Older posts also quote Humana: "they don't support non face to face enrollments" and "if an agent is NOT contracted & certified as a TELESALES agent, he/she should NOT enroll clients over the phone." (Carrier rules change; treat as historical/anecdotal.) — [insurance-forums, Medicare Advantage Marketing Compliance p.4](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/medicare-advantage-marketing-compliance.108941/page-4); [insurance-forums, How are face 2 face agents planning on handling this p.3](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/how-are-face-2-face-agents-planning-on-handling-this.110928/page-3); [insurance-forums, Selling Med Sups over the phone p.4](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/selling-med-sups-over-the-phone.74094/page-4)
- **[Forum]** Thread "How to conduct a compliant MAPD phone presentation/enrollment": one Healthspring/Cigna process described — call must be inbound and recorded, agent gets verbal permission to present, gives full presentation, then gets verbal permission to enroll. Another veteran advises not to enroll by phone "unless the prospect is serious and you have a PTC [permission to contact] and Scope," to check all pre-enrollment steps and keep good notes. Several agents avoid recorded phone enrollment entirely by meeting face to face and sending the e-enrollment link afterward. One poster's suggestion to skip disclosures on the call was challenged by others. — [insurance-forums thread 117335](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/how-to-conduct-a-compliant-mapd-phone-presentation-enrollment.117335/)
- **[Carrier doc]** Clover Health Telephonic Assisted Enrollment QRG (Sept 2021): a TAE representative "utilizes recorded phone line and asks a series of questions of both agent and client," then "provides a voice signature code that the agent enters on the signature line in lieu of the client's signature"; all calls recorded. Missing a step (no code) is a plausible return reason. — [Clover Health TAE QRG (PDF)](https://cdn.cloverhealth.com/filer_public/cd/ac/cdacb0fb-63d7-485a-8240-df427d677e07/telephonic_assisted_enrollment_qrg_ch_010_092021.pdf)
- **[GA doc]** GoldenCare Voice Signature Guide (Nov 2025): confirm the client is comfortable being recorded before proceeding; "agents should review all plan benefits with the client before enrolling, since CMS requires that"; recordings stored 10 years. — [GoldenCare Voice Signature Guide (PDF)](https://www.goldencareagent.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/VoiceSignatureGuide.pdf)
- **[Vendor training]** EnrollHere: a voice signature "serves as the legal equivalent of a written signature for Medicare Advantage (MA) enrollments completed via telephone"; agents must confirm required data before asking for the signature; script should be CMS-approved. — [EnrollHere Academy, Voice Signature](https://academy.enrollhere.com/Voice-Signature-1afbe273c76380d89f0fc32ae9160f77)

**Consent for data sharing (newer flag)**
- **[FMO blog]** Since Oct 1, 2024 TPMOs must obtain prior express written consent before sharing a beneficiary's personal data with another TPMO. — [Quotit, Common Mistakes for Insurance Agents During AEP and OEP](https://hub.quotit.com/blog/common-mistakes-for-insurance-agents-to-avoid-during-aep-and-oep)

### Inferences
- The "kick-back" list splits into two layers: (a) carrier-side pends for data/election-period defects (fixable, mostly delay commission), and (b) compliance flags (no SOA, no/partial recording, missing disclaimer, wrong channel) that can void the enrollment or generate a CTM/sales allegation. Practitioners worry more about (b) because it threatens contracts, not just one commission.
- Forum discussion shows carrier policy — not CMS — is the practical gatekeeper on *whether* an independent agent can phone-enroll at all and *how* (carrier TAE line, carrier e-app with voice signature, or agent's own recording). "Which carrier process?" is the right first question.

### Gaps
- No carrier-published pend/reject reason codes or "not in good order" statistics were found; no CMS data on rejection frequency by cause.
- Nothing found documenting an "agent answering for the beneficiary" rule or examples (searched directly; zero hits). It appears as training lore rather than written guidance in accessible sources.
- Full text of Ritter's tips 2–10 could not be retrieved.

## Key Question 2: Habits that make the call short and simple while staying compliant

### Takeaway
Practitioner advice converges on: prep everything before you dial (SOA/PTC on file, client data incl. MBI already in CRM/e-app), run a fixed opening script (recording notice + consent, identity, TPMO disclaimer), present only what's on the SOA, use the carrier e-app/voice-signature flow, read back data before signature, and download/store the recording immediately.

### Cited Findings
- **[FMO blog]** Ritter: have full name, contact info, DOB and MBI "all in one place" before starting; use a CRM; submit online rather than paper; send prefilled apps for e-signature. — [Ritter, 10 Tips](https://ritterim.com/blog/10-tips-to-submitting-an-error-free-medicare-enrollment-application/)
- **[Vendor blog]** Required opening elements: agent's name and license status, agency name, every carrier that may be discussed, a statement the agent is not connected to the federal government, and notice the call is recorded. Sample recording line: the call "is being recorded for quality and compliance, and that continuing the call means consenting to it." — [AgentTech, CMS-Compliant Enrollment Calls](https://agenttech.io/blog/cms-compliant-enrollment-calls); [AgentTech, Medicare Compliance Guide (Jan 22, 2026)](https://agenttech.io/blog/medicare-compliance-guide)
- **[Carrier/GA doc]** Warner Pacific checklist: confirm recording and get verbal agreement; disclaimer within 60 seconds; give name, licensed title, agency; "don't imply you're from Medicare." — [Warner Pacific checklist](https://login.warnerpacific.com/getmedia/7f54fcd2-0365-408b-a279-5f82a168c8e5/Broker-Checklist-Phone-and-Enrollments.pdf)
- **[FMO blog]** New Horizons: outbound calls require documented permission (business reply card, web form, SOA process, or other recorded request); agents can call current enrollees, return calls, and call prospects who gave permission, but not make unsolicited calls. — [New Horizons, 10 MA Appointment Tips](https://blog.newhorizonsmktg.com/medicare-advantage-appointments-101-how-to-stay-compliant2984); [New Horizons, How to Sell Medicare Advantage Over the Phone (Aug 11, 2026)](https://blog.newhorizonsmktg.com/how-to-sell-medicare-advantage-over-the-phone7895)
- **[Vendor blog]** Post-enrollment check: have the beneficiary confirm their choice and personal information on the recording (vendor best practice, not CMS text). Audit-readiness items: call recordings, SOA documentation, agent training records. — [AgentTech, CMS Audit Readiness Checklist (Mar 14, 2026)](https://agenttech.io/blog/cms-audit-readiness-checklist)
- **[Vendor blog]** Assume two-party consent and announce recording at the start of every conversation; keep recordings in .WAV/.MP3, retrievable on request. — [PSM Brokerage, Call Recording and Retention](https://www.psmbrokerage.com/blog/call-recording-and-retention-requirements-for-telephonic-sales-of-medicare-plans); [NIMBroker, Call Recording Requirements (Jul 9, 2025)](https://nimbroker.com/2025/07/09/call-recording-medicare/)
- **[Forum]** Download recordings before your phone system purges them (90-day example). — [insurance-forums, Call recording ridiculousness p.8](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/call-recording-ridiculousness.108899/page-8)
- **[Forum]** Many field agents simply avoid recorded phone enrollments: meet face to face (no recording required), then send the enrollment link. — [insurance-forums thread 117335](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/how-to-conduct-a-compliant-mapd-phone-presentation-enrollment.117335/)
- **[FMO blog]** New Horizons: "one of the most common mistakes agents make during AEP" is waiting too long to review plan changes; review ANOCs for premium, formulary, network changes before calls. — [New Horizons, Medicare Agent's Guide to AEP (Sep 8, 2026)](https://blog.newhorizonsmktg.com/medicare-agents-guide-to-aep)

**CY2027 rule changes that shorten the process (effective for marketing Oct 1, 2026)**
- **[FMO/Vendor blogs]** The 48-hour SOA waiting period (42 CFR 422.2264(c)(3)/423.2264(c)(3)) is eliminated; beginning Oct 1, 2026 agents can go straight from a signed SOA to the personal marketing appointment, but the SOA still must be completed before the appointment starts. Rule published Apr 6, 2026 (CMS-4208-F3), regulatory effective date June 1, 2026. The 12-hour educational-to-sales gap is also removed per one podcast. — [Agent CRM](https://blog.agent-crm.com/medicare-final-rule-for-plan-year-2027-everything-agents-need-to-know/); [New Horizons](https://blog.newhorizonsmktg.com/the-2027-cms-final-rule-what-agents-need-to-know); [Action Benefits, CMS removes barriers to selling](https://blog.actionbenefits.com/cms-removes-barriers-to-selling-with-2027-medicare-final-rule); [Insurance Producers Guild podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2603798/episodes/19522040-ep19-2027-medicare-rules-faster-sales-more-liability); [Cal Broker Magazine, Calvin Bagley (Dec 2025)](https://www.calbrokermag.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-2027-Final-Rule-and-Medicare-MAPD-by-Calvin-Bagley.pdf)
- **[Vendor blogs]** Recording retention: marketing/sales calls now 6 years (first 3 years audio; years 4–6 audio or complete, accurate transcript) per amended 42 CFR 422.2274(g)(2)(ii)/423.2274(g)(2)(ii); enrollment call recordings remain on a 10-year track because "the enrollment portion must still be recorded because it can serve as the enrollment form and proof of intent." — [Tech Savvy Insurance](https://techsavvyinsurance.com/blog/medicare-call-recording-retention-2027/); [InformedPlusChoice, CY2027 Call Recording](https://informedpluschoice.com/blog/medicare-call-recording-requirements-agents-cy2027/); [Carecycle, 2027 Final Rule](https://carecycle.ai/blog/cms-2027-final-rule-medicare-agents)
- **Conflict:** Senior Benefit Services and Ritter's 2027 summary say "only calls that result in an enrollment must be recorded"; InformedPlusChoice and Carecycle say the duty to record marketing/sales calls is unchanged and only retention shortened. Unresolved without the rule text. — [Senior Benefit Services](https://srbenefit.com/the-cms-2027-final-rule-key-updates-every-medicare-agent-should-understand/); [Ritter 2027 rule post](https://ritterim.com/blog/cms-improves-plan-transparency-and-choice-with-2027-ma-and-part-d-final-rule); vs. [InformedPlusChoice](https://informedpluschoice.com/blog/marketing-call-vs-enrollment-recording-medicare-agents/)

### Inferences
- A practical "short call" checklist assembled from the above: (1) PTC + SOA on file before dialing; (2) client's Medicare card data (MBI, Part A/B dates), address, DOB, phone, Medicaid/LIS status, PCP and Rx list pre-loaded in CRM/e-app; (3) opening script: recording notice + verbal OK, name/license/agency, carriers represented, "not Medicare/federal government," TPMO disclaimer before benefits; (4) stick to SOA products; (5) required benefit review (premium, Part B premium, copays, network, formulary, LEP); (6) read back data, confirm election period + effective date, voice signature per carrier flow; (7) save recording same day to long-term storage with client name/date.
- Because carriers' telephonic flows differ (TAE line vs. e-app voice signature vs. agent's own recording), the simplest habit is to standardize on 1–2 carriers' flows the agent knows cold.

### Gaps
- No practitioner source quantified call length or gave "keep it under X minutes" advice; the content is about sequence, not time.
- Cody Askins / Christopher Westfall / Medicare Agent Training YouTube transcripts: no results surfaced via search; could not fetch YouTube.

## Key Question 3: How brokers handle Spanish-language calls and recordings

### Takeaway
No practitioner or official source found that specifically addresses recording Spanish-language telephonic enrollments by independent agents (interpreter use, bilingual recording, translation of recordings). The only related official material is CMS plan-level language-access guidance and CMS's complaint-translation memo; vendor blogs note that consent/disclaimer wording must be properly translated and that interpreters on three-way calls must remain on the recording.

### Cited Findings
- **[Official, old]** CMS LEP best-practices memo (2008): plans should have interpreters/translation available for non-English beneficiaries, contract with an interpretation vendor for languages reps don't speak, and track preferred language in the call record. — [CMS LEP best practices memo (PDF)](https://www.hhs.gov/guidance/sites/default/files/hhs-guidance-documents/CMS/memolepbestpractices_01.02.08.pdf)
- **[Official, old]** CMS 2006 memo: Part D sponsors must translate non-English CTM complaint notes into English (complaints, not enrollment calls). — [CMS complaint translation memo (PDF)](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/prescription-drug-coverage/prescriptiondrugcovcontra/downloads/memocomplainttranslation_111606.pdf)
- **[Official]** CMS monitors plan call centers for interpreter availability/accuracy in six languages including Spanish; compliance action possible when interpreter availability is below 75%; 10-minute max to reach a live rep. — [LanguageLine summary of CMS Accuracy & Accessibility Study](https://www.languageline.com/blog/three-key-requirements-of-the-cms-accuracy-and-accessibility-study)
- **[Agent-training site]** MA orgs must translate required marketing/enrollment materials into any non-English language that is the primary language of ≥5% of the service area; for CY2026 CMS replaced the Multi-Language Insert with an expanded Notice of Availability of Language Assistance Services. — [Open Exam Prep, 11.2 Marketing to Vulnerable Populations](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ahip-medicare/marketing-conduct-and-oversight/vulnerable-populations-non-discrimination)
- **[Vendor blog]** Recording must continue through conference/three-way calls (which is how an interpreter would be added); agents remain responsible for "carrier procedures, agency policies, state recording-consent laws, privacy requirements, approved scripts." — [PSM Brokerage](https://www.psmbrokerage.com/blog/call-recording-and-retention-requirements-for-telephonic-sales-of-medicare-plans); [Thunai](https://www.thunai.ai/blog/cms-call-recording-requirements)
- **[Vendor blog]** AgentTech published an "Agency Spanish Bilingual Strategy" piece (Jul 18, 2026) — unreachable; content not verified. — [AgentTech, Agency Spanish Bilingual Strategy](https://agenttech.io/blog/agency-spanish-bilingual-strategy)
- **[Forum]** A bilingual NJ agent argues bilingual agents fill a gap for Spanish-speaking seniors (opinion thread on commissions; no process detail). — [insurance-forums thread 117836](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/aoc-spoke-about-medicare-agents-she-never-spoke-to-one-aoc-came-for-my-profession-sunday-she-waved-my-flag.117836/)
- **[Official]** CMS FAQ: recording applies to "all organizations and individuals that fall under the definition of TPMO" — no language exception is mentioned. — [CMS FAQs 10-19-2022](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/agent-broker-marketing-faqs-10-19-2022.pdf)

### Inferences
- Since CMS rules contain no language carve-out, a Spanish-language recorded call must contain the same elements (recording consent, TPMO disclaimer verbatim in the carrier-approved Spanish version, SOA, benefit review, voice signature). Carriers typically supply Spanish-language approved scripts/disclaimers; agents should use those rather than ad-lib translations.
- Three-way interpreter calls are compliant if the interpreter is on the recording for the whole call and the beneficiary (not the interpreter or family member) gives the attestations.

### Gaps
- No source found on: whether carriers require English transcripts of Spanish recordings; whether family members may interpret; carrier-specific Spanish voice-signature scripts; any practitioner discussion of Spanish TAE lines. The writer should flag this as unverified and recommend checking carrier broker portals.

## Key Question 4: Real audit / secret-shopper findings on TPMO calls

### Takeaway
The only quantified call-review finding remains CMS's Oct 2022 memo (>80% of reviewed calls had missing/inaccurate information). CMS's 2024 rule requires plans to secret-shop and randomly sample agent recordings, and CMS itself secret-shops numbers tied to ads. No CMS-published 2024–2026 secret-shopper statistics for agent calls were found; the only newer "secret shopper" item is an Agility (FMO) summary of a Humana internal survey listing what agents failed to do at marketing events, without percentages. A July 2026 GAO report on unauthorized enrollments concerns the ACA Marketplace, not Medicare.

### Cited Findings
- **[Official via trade press]** CMS Oct 2022 memo: reviewed thousands of complaints and hundreds of audio calls; agents failed to give necessary information or gave inaccurate information on more than 80% of calls reviewed; concerns included confusing/misleading info and undue pressure; CMS reminded plans they are responsible for agents' and TPMOs' marketing. — [AIS Health, CMS Spooks MA Plans With Warning About Secret Shopping](https://aishealth.mmitnetwork.com/blogs/radar-on-medicare-advantage/cms-spooks-ma-plans-with-warning-about-secret-shopping-enhanced-monitoring); [Healthcare Finance News](https://www.healthcarefinancenews.com/news/cms-cracks-down-misleading-medicare-advantage-marketing); [Sheppard Mullin](https://www.sheppard.com/insights/blogs/cms-heightens-oversight-of-tpmo-marketing-programs-restricts-tv-advertisements)
- **[Official]** CMS FAQs: "CMS employs secret shoppers who then call numbers associated with TV ads, newspaper ads, mailings, and internet searches to observe the beneficiary experience." — [CMS FAQs 10-19-2022](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/agent-broker-marketing-faqs-10-19-2022.pdf)

### Inferences
- For an independent agent, the realistic audit trigger is a CTM/sales-allegation complaint leading the carrier to pull the recording and SOA; random carrier sampling is the second path. What gets checked, per the above: recording exists and is complete, disclaimer present and verbatim, SOA covers product, required benefit items (premium, Part B premium, LEP, network) explained, no pressure, beneficiary's own attestations.
